| AI spend       | separate $5/site and $50/platform draft hard caps                     | final cap and paid-provider authorization                   |

ORAP can change versioned plan assignment, subscription status, grace date, feature overrides, emergency controls and AI cap with an audit reason. The effective-settings view distinguishes plan values from overrides. No plan, delinquency or suspension state removes an already published static website; it can stop new privileged operations while the public artifact remains served.

Payment-provider automation is **Deferred**. Billing states are administrative records, not evidence of collection or authorization to charge.
