# Cookie and Analytics Notice — draft

> **DRAFT — NOT APPROVED — DO NOT PUBLISH**

OmniReach uses essential browser storage for security, sessions, and remembering the
analytics choice. With consent, GA4 may set or read analytics cookies and receive page,
device, referral, and interaction data to produce aggregate reporting. Advertising
storage, ad personalization, and ad user-data signals remain denied.

The current consent control defaults analytics storage to denied before the Google tag
initializes. “Accept analytics” enables analytics; “Decline” keeps it denied and attempts
to remove accessible first-party `_ga` cookies. A decline cannot recall data sent before
the choice or delete provider records. Users must be given a persistent way to reopen
preferences.

Before approval, attach an observed production inventory listing each cookie/storage
key, owner, purpose, category, lifetime, domain, and whether it is set before consent.
Legal review must approve the treatment of Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track and
any region-specific opt-in/opt-out behavior.
