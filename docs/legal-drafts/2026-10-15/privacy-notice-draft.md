## Information and purposes

We may process information a person submits in an inquiry, such as name, business,
email, optional phone number, and message, to respond and maintain business records. Do
not submit passwords, payment-card data, health data, government identifiers, or other
unnecessary sensitive information.

With the visitor’s analytics choice, the public site uses GA4 to measure traffic and page
use. Essential security and preference data may be processed without analytics consent
to remember a choice, protect forms, prevent abuse, maintain sessions, and operate the
site. Managed customer reporting may separately process read-only Search Console and GA4
data after an authorized Google account connects a verified property.

Account and service operation may process identity, authentication, authorization,
security, content, publication, usage, billing-status, support, audit, and operational
records. Passwords use Argon2id hashing; one-time verification material is hashed or
encrypted as appropriate. Security records may be retained where necessary to prevent
fraud, investigate incidents, and preserve an audit trail.

## Retention and security

Customer contact submissions have a 30-day application retention target unless a
documented legal or security hold applies. Platform inquiries are delivered to active
Platform Owners and are not stored as inquiry bodies in the application database;
provider and recipient mailboxes may retain them under approved business-email policy.
Operational logs default to 30 days, detailed job records to 90 days, and audit records
to 12 months. Content revisions follow the site policy and are not silently deleted.
Encrypted backups retain three daily and one weekly recovery point. **[APPROVE EXACT
RETENTION, HOLDS, MAILBOX RULES, TOKEN/METRIC RETENTION, AND BACKUP-DELETION EFFECT.]**

We use access controls, tenant isolation, encrypted transport, secret separation, audit
records, rate limits, and tested backups. No system can be promised absolutely secure.
Incidents are handled under the internal response plan; **[APPROVE EXTERNAL NOTICE
STANDARD AND CONTACT METHOD]**.
