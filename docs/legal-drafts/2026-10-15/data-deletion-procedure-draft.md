# Data Deletion and Export Procedure — draft

> **DRAFT — NOT APPROVED — DO NOT OPERATE OR PUBLISH**

1. Receive requests only through **[APPROVED CHANNEL]** and create a restricted case.
2. Verify the requestor’s identity and, for customer data, their authority for the site
   and tenant. Never use password recovery to bypass required privileged verification.
3. Inventory applicable data by category and controller: account, content/revisions,
   media, submissions, metrics/OAuth, audit/security, logs/jobs, email provider/mailbox,
   live artifacts, and encrypted backups.
4. Determine access, correction, export, deletion, restriction, legal/security hold,
   contractual handoff, and statutory obligations with legal review. Record the basis;
   do not silently delete immutable audit or evidence records.
5. Obtain owner authorization for any manual backup deletion. Automated pruning may occur
   only under the approved three-daily/one-weekly policy. Deleted live data can remain in
   encrypted backups until those recovery points age out and must not be restored except
   for authorized recovery, followed by reapplication of the deletion.
6. Execute tenant-scoped actions with least privilege, two-person review where required,
   provider propagation, before/after counts, and no sensitive values in logs.
7. Respond within **[LEGAL DEADLINE/INTERNAL TARGET]**, state completed categories and
   lawful exceptions, provide **[APPEAL/ESCALATION PROCESS]**, and retain the restricted
   case record for **[APPROVED PERIOD]**.
